    ROM needed

    The emulator needs the original ROM: 48.rom (16,384 bytes) for the 48K, 128.rom (32,768 bytes) for the 128K. Drop it here, or press EJECT on the recorder to open it; it is remembered in this browser.

    1. Using the emulator

    This booklet is for anyone who has never used a ZX Spectrum, and for those who used one long ago and have forgotten how. Everything on the desk works: the computer, the television, the recorder and the gadgets around them.

    1. 1The computer. Choose it with the television's channel buttons: 48K, + or 128. Changing channel resets it.
    2. 2A tape. Press STOP/EJ on the recorder and pick a .tap or .tzx tape, a .z80 or .sna snapshot, or a ROM. You can also drop files anywhere on the page.
    3. 3The recorder. With AUTO LOAD on, the tape starts by itself. Otherwise type the load command and press PLAY.

    Loading a program by hand

    On the 48K and the Spectrum+, type

    LOAD ""
    (J, then SYMBOL SHIFT + P twice, then ENTER)

    and press PLAY. The border flashes while the program loads. On the 128, choose Tape Loader from the menu instead.

    Click the cassette to see what is on the tape; click a line to wind the tape to it.

    If something goes wrong

    Press the reset button on the side of the computer. The Multiface's red button stops a game so that you can save it and come back later.

    2. The desk

    Everything you need is on the desk. The screen is laid out thus:

    1. 1Multiface One. The red button freezes the game and opens its menu on the television.
    2. 2Television. Double-click the picture for full screen.
    3. 3Channel buttons 48K, + and 128 choose the computer; VOLUME, COLOUR, ZOOM and POWER.
    4. 4Joystick. Click it to plug it in.
    5. 5This manual.
    6. 6ZX Spectrum. Type on your PC keyboard or click the keys.
    7. 7Reset button on the side of the computer.
    8. 8Datacorder. STOP/EJ opens a tape; click the cassette to see what is on it.

    Rest the mouse on any of them to see its name. On a phone they are arranged to fit the screen.

    3. The keyboard

    Each Spectrum key does several jobs, printed on and around it. On your PC keyboard the letters, numbers and ENTER work as you would expect; the others are:

    Left ShiftCAPS SHIFT
    Right Shift CtrlSYMBOL SHIFT
    BackspaceDELETE (CAPS SHIFT + 0)
    EscBREAK (CAPS SHIFT + SPACE)
    TabExtended mode (CAPS + SYMBOL)
    ← ↑ → ↓Cursor keys, or the joystick when it is plugged in (Left Alt = fire)
    , . ; ' - = /SYMBOL SHIFT + N M O P J L V

    You can also click or tap the keys on the screen. A click on CAPS SHIFT or SYMBOL SHIFT holds it down for the next key. The Spectrum+ and 128 keys such as DELETE, EDIT, the arrows and ; " , . press the two keys they stand for.

    4. Settings

    SPEED switchNORMAL: real tape speed, with the border stripes and the loading sound. FAST: the computer runs flat out while the tape plays. INSTANT: programs go straight into memory.
    AUTO LOADPutting in a tape resets the computer and starts loading it (LOAD "" on the 48K and Spectrum+, Tape Loader on the 128).
    CounterShows how far the tape has played; the small button sets it to 000.
    MultifaceThe red button freezes the game: SAVE downloads a snapshot, LOAD opens one, TURBO runs as fast as possible, RESET restarts, RETURN carries on.
    JoystickClick it to plug it in: the arrow keys and Left Alt then work a Kempston joystick.
    TelevisionZOOM (or a double-click on the picture) fills the screen. Drag the knobs for volume and colour; POWER pauses everything.

    5. About

    An emulator of the Sinclair ZX Spectrum 48K (1982), Spectrum+ (1984) and Spectrum 128 (1986), written in plain JavaScript. It runs in the browser, with nothing to install.

    The Spectrum ROMs are copyright Amstrad, who allow them to be distributed for use with emulators.
